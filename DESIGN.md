## Overview

Reconstructed draft. The site is a sportswear store whose home page leads with a full-bleed campaign image and a condensed, heavy uppercase headline, followed by horizontally scrolling product rows.

## Typography

Headlines use a condensed display face in uppercase with tight leading (line-height below font size); body, navigation, buttons, prices and product titles share one neutral sans at the body size.

## Layout

The header overlays the hero: category links on the left, wordmark centred, icon actions on the right. Product rows sit under a section title with a "View All" link and use near-zero gutters between cards.

## Shapes

Buttons and product images have square corners; primary buttons on imagery are solid white with dark text, the secondary button is a white outline.

## Components

Product cards show a 4:5 image on a light grey surface with a wishlist icon top-right and an optional label chip bottom-left, then title, muted variant line, and price.
